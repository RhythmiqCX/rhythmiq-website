# Who we reach out to

The sender picks the niche for each batch. ray is working through
technical-leaning niches one at a time, so never assume a vertical.

Go after companies whose people actually use LinkedIn: B2B services,
logistics, real estate brokerages, recruitment, SaaS and startups, e-commerce
brands, consultancies, fintech and proptech. Local shops like cafés, salons and
clinics mostly aren't on LinkedIn. Those belong to the Maps scraper in
rhythmiq-leads-scripts, not this skill.

Every lead gets one of two tracks, based on their title and company. If it's
unclear, ask.

## Track A: small companies (about 5 to 200 people)

**Who:** founder, owner, CEO, COO, head of operations, CTO or tech lead.

**Public signs there's work for us:**
- Job posts for developers, "automation", operations coordinators, data entry,
  receptionists or call handling. They're paying people for work software
  could take on, or they need more build capacity.
- Manual steps on their site: "email us to book", PDF forms, "call for a
  quote", nothing that answers after hours.
- A site or product that's clearly behind: slow, broken on mobile, no
  self-serve option.
- The person's own posts, if pasted: a problem, a launch, hiring, growth.
- Reviews that mention slow replies, missed calls or hard booking.

**Offer shape:** one specific fix, fixed price.

## Track B: agencies (about 2 to 50 people)

**Who:** founder, owner, managing director or head of delivery at a web,
marketing, design or dev agency.

**Public signs there's work for us:**
- Their portfolio or case studies: what those clients would gain from an AI
  assistant, a voice receptionist or an automation.
- Signs they're stretched: hiring developers, "we're growing" posts, long
  delivery times.
- No AI on their services page, while their clients are probably asking for it.

**Offer shape:** we build the AI piece, or take their overflow dev work, under
their name, and they resell it. One agency can mean many end clients.

**Demo idea that works for agencies:** build it for one of their public
portfolio clients, so they see exactly what they could sell.
